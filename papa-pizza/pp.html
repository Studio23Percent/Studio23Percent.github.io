Papa Pizza Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Papa Pizza (the "Game"), developed by 23% (the "Developer", "we", "us"), handles your information.

In short: Papa Pizza does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we do not collect

The Game does not ask for, collect, or transmit:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details;
  • your location;
  • contacts, photos, files, or other content on your device;
  • advertising identifiers or device identifiers;
  • usage analytics or behavioral tracking data.

The Game contains no advertising, no analytics services, and no in-app purchases, and it does not require an account or an internet connection to play.

Game progress

Your game progress (for example, money, purchased items, and storage levels) is saved only locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data permanently deletes this progress.

Third-party services

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect certain information (such as crash reports or installation statistics) according to its own policies. We have no control over this data. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 or any other age group.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us
